Reading this list

Ranking limitations. Three included providers currently measure Reddit attention, with different communities and counting rules. Their overlap is intentionally retained. Non-Reddit sources were checked but did not supply usable current raw counts in this run. Provider top-list omissions are N/A, not zero. ChartExchange coverage was limited to the retrievable table and last-24-hour jump widgets. A low score can reflect unavailable coverage. Common-word symbols, especially WTF, CIA, HE, DC, DEI and LINK, can produce false matches. These are provider-reported counts, not independently audited stock-specific posts.

Eligibility. Only the verified common/ordinary-share candidates priced below $10 were ranked. ETFs, preferred shares, warrants, units, OTC and unverified listings were excluded. ADR/ADS candidates such as IQ and LOT were left outside this strict common/ordinary-share pass. This is not a complete exchange-wide census, and absent/lower-ranked providers may contain other leaders.

Price timestamps. The quote feed's latest November 12 trade snapshots are shown in Pacific time for each stock. All listed times fall in the extended-hours period; prices may differ from closing prices, other venues and current executable orders. Each Yahoo Finance link opens the current reference page. Source-window end times vary; “retrieved” is not a claimed provider update time.

Targets and action. The window is November 13–19, 2026, ending November 19 at regular-session close. Target change = (target ÷ scan price − 1) × 100. The PLCE $0.38 target is a conditional retest of the quote feed's November 12 intraday low, not a guaranteed forecast; a sharp rebound invalidates the immediate downside setup. Sell is a research exit view for existing holdings, not a recommendation to short. The other rows lack a defensible weekly target and show Unavailable. No qualifying Buy was established. High attention does not determine direction.

Baseline. No valid historical relative-mention baseline was supplied for this run. Uploaded test exports contained unavailable counts, so no relative multiplier was invented.
